# Source Rights, Evidence and Verification Policy

## Non-negotiables
- Prefer venue-owned websites, official event pages, authenticated venue submissions and explicitly licensed feeds.
- No indiscriminate social scraping, bypassing login/CAPTCHA, automated access that violates source restrictions, or storing media without rights.
- Maintain source-specific terms, retention, attribution, regional restrictions and permitted use. Consult counsel before public deployment; this document is not legal advice.
- Treat Google Places and other third-party APIs as licensed services with limits on storage, caching and attribution. Provider IDs may be stored where permitted; do not assume other fields are permanently retainable.
- Keep independent first-party claims separate from licensed provider payloads.

## Evidence states
`unknown`: no credible evidence; `source_claimed`: one attributed source; `corroborated`: consistent independent sources; `verified`: accepted verification method completed; `conflicting`: credible disagreement; `stale`: evidence past policy validity; `rejected`: incorrect or prohibited.

## Verification methods
Authenticated operator attestation; current official event publication; independently checked venue communication; reviewed first-party observation; authorized structured provider feed. Each method records verifier, scope and date. A source can confirm its own declared programme without independently proving actual delivery.

## Attribute freshness (pilot defaults, configurable)
Opening hours 7 days; event listings 24 hours, shorter close to event; menus/prices 30 days; static amenities 90 days; atmosphere observations 30 days with sample-size indicator. Expiry downgrades confidence; do not delete historical evidence.

## Quality and bias
Explain why a match is recommended and list unknowns. Sponsored results always labeled and cannot violate hard constraints. User reviews are moderated; ratings with small samples show uncertainty rather than being suppressed until 100 reviews.

## Privacy
Minimal personal data, explicit notification opt-in, deletion/export processes, venue operator permissions, data retention and access logging. Do not collect precise location unless needed and consented. Evaluate UAE and each new market's privacy law, consumer advertising rules and event data licensing before activation.

## Source registry fields
provider, connector, base domains, market permissions, permitted endpoints, allowed fields, storage policy, retention TTL, attribution requirements, robots/TOS review date, cost model, legal reviewer, activation status, kill switch.
